A metronome cannot teach you compás.

Flamenco Compás Master is a refined flamenco rhythm companion that replaces sterile metronome clicks with the living, breathing pulse of real palmas and cajón.

Every palmas and cajón sample recorded by hand by Doruk Okuyucu

Rhythm in flamenco is more than counting beats, it’s about feeling the compás. Flamenco Compás Master is built for dancers, guitarists, and singers who want a practice tool that feels alive. Instead of relying on the robotic, synthetic clicks of a traditional metronome, it uses real palmas and cajón recordings to create a natural, musical groove. It is designed as a beautifully minimal, distraction free space that helps artists truly internalize the heartbeat of flamenco and focus purely on their craft.

Real palmas, not clicks

Recorded claras, sordas and cajón one-shots, scheduled on the audio clock so the compás never drifts.

Twelve palos

Soleá, bulería, tangos, siguiriyas, alegrías, fandangos, rumba, sevillanas, tientos and more, each with its own accents.

Practice, not production

Balance palmas against cajón, set the tempo, and nothing else. No accounts, no ads, works offline.
